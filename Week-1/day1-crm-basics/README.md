# Day 1: CRM Basics & Salesforce Fundamentals

## 1. What is CRM?

CRM stands for **Customer Relationship Management**. It is a technology that helps businesses manage relationships with their customers and prospects. CRM stores all customer interactions, tracks data, helps teams work together, and allows companies to communicate through email, phone, social media, and other channels. In simple words, CRM helps businesses understand and serve their customers better.

## 2. Why companies use Salesforce

Companies use Salesforce because:

- **It's a customer success platform** – helps sell, service, market, and connect with customers from anywhere
- **It organizes data in the cloud** – secure, accessible from desktop and mobile
- **It's customizable** – companies can add custom fields, objects, and apps without writing code
- **It has trusted values** – Salesforce puts trust, customer success, innovation, equality, and sustainability first
- **It's a platform for change** – follows the 1-1-1 model (1% time, 1% equity, 1% product given back to communities)

## 3. Key Salesforce Terms Explained

| Term | Definition | Meaning |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Account** | A company you're doing business with | The organization/customer |
| **Contact** | A person who works at an account | The individual person |
| **Opportunity** | A qualified lead that is ready to buy | A potential deal/sale |

## 4. Real-World Mapping

**Chosen scenario: College Admission**

| Salesforce Concept | Real-World Mapping |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Account** | The College/University |
| **Contact** | The Student applicant |
| **Lead** | A prospective student who has shown interest (requested brochure, attended campus tour) |
| **Opportunity** | A submitted application (potential enrollment) |

### Business Flow: Lead → Customer

**In my own words:**

1. **Lead** – A prospective student asks for information about admission
2. **Contact** – The student fills out an application and becomes a contact
3. **Opportunity** – The application is reviewed (potential enrollment)
4. **Customer** – The student gets accepted and enrolls in the college

## Bonus: What is Trailhead?

Trailhead is Salesforce's **free learning platform** with interactive modules, badges, and hands-on challenges. It provides:
- Trailhead Playground (a safe practice environment)
- Step-by-step learning paths
- Real-world hands-on challenges

## What is a Salesforce Developer?

A Salesforce Developer builds solutions for business problems using:
- Low-code tools (Flow Builder)
- Programming languages (Apex, JavaScript for Lightning Web Components)
- APIs and integrations
- They need both **technical skills** and **soft skills** (communication, problem-solving)
